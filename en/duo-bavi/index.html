Duo BAVI is a duo from Salvador, Brazil, that takes the berimbau and the guitar into electronic music with beats, samples and live looping, turning samba-chula, maracatu, lambada and cumbia into dance-floor music.

Track record

  • 2017 · Finalist at the Prêmio Caymmi de Música (Best Show and Breakthrough Artist). At the awards ceremony, joined the Bandão Caymmi with 37 Bahian artists, Saulo and Alice Caymmi
  • 2018 · Atlantikaldia Festival, Errenteria (Basque Country), with shows by the duo and by BAVIAI, a collective with singer Aiace, and Festival Radioca (Salvador)
  • 2020 · Planet Afropunk, online festival
  • 2020 · Sync on Canal OFF: tracks from the EP Que Onda é Essa? in the soundtrack of the OFF Docs episode “Bob Burnquist voando em Morro de São Paulo!”
  • Funarte · Artist residency through the Bolsa Funarte de Música – Pixinguinha grant
  • 2026 · 10 Anos Dessa Onda tour: Centro Cultural Banco do Nordeste (Fortaleza, CE), Festival de Artes de São Cristóvão – FASC (SE) and Salvador (BA)
