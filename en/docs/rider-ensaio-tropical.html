Jalmy’s audio outputs

Four channels of beats and samples: one stereo pair (L/R) and two independent channels.

P.A. and monitoring

Mixing console with at least 24 inputs and 5 monitor aux sends. P.A. sized for the venue. One independent monitor mix per musician preferred.

Stage layout

V-shaped setup with Jalmy front and center. Lucas on one side; Ágatha and Carol Pêpa on the opposite side. Drums upstage center.

Infrastructure and stage support

Grounded power with safe distribution near the electronics, guitar and bass. Sturdy table or stand for controllers and sample gear, instrument stands, mic stands, and suitable space and seating for percussion and drums.

To be confirmed with production: lineup for each date, percussion and drum setup, matching mics, and guitarra baiana pickup.
