Jalmy is a singer, songwriter and music producer from Salvador, Brazil. In Ensaio Tropical he blends ijexá, samba-reggae and arrocha with cumbia and salsa, through acoustic guitar, guitarra baiana, beats and spoken word. The show is a journey through the rhythms, stories and places of Latin America, honoring tradition and carrying it into the present.

Milestones

  • 2012 · Mini-tour in Nairobi, Kenya, with Camila Jatobá e Banda de Bolso
  • 2017 · Duo BAVI finalist at the Prêmio Caymmi (Best Show and Breakthrough Artist)
  • 2018 · Atlantikaldia Festival (Basque Country) and Festival Radioca (Salvador), with Duo BAVI
  • 2023 · Jalmy em Movimento vol. II, Sala do Coro, Teatro Castro Alves
  • 2025 · 7th Mostra Sesc de Música · finalist at the Festival Geraldo Azevedo (Petrolina) · Baile de Movimento, Selo Educadora Independente
  • 2026 · Jalmy em Movimento de Verão, Sesc Pelourinho (finalist) · Ensaio Tropical, Teatro Gamboa Nova
